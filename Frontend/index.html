About the university

A university in the hills, built around its students.

Graphic Era Hill University was founded in 2011 as a private university in Dehradun, Uttarakhand. Today it teaches across three campuses — Dehradun, Bhimtal and Haldwani — in engineering, computer applications, management and other disciplines.

Admission is based on merit in the qualifying examination, and the application is completed online.

Admissions

Admissions are open for 2026.

Applications are assessed on merit in the qualifying examination and completed online.

  1. 1

    Apply online

    Submit the application with your academic details, preferred program and supporting documents.

  2. 2

    Verification

    The Admissions Office checks your documents and may ask for anything missing. You can track the status with your tracking ID.

  3. 3

    Enrolment

    On confirmation you complete enrolment, attend orientation and begin the session.
